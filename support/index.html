Game support

Questions, feedback, or a tricky technical problem? Contact Tiago Santos at whiteotterlabs@gmail.com.

Reporting a problem

Please include your device model, iOS or Android version, the game version, the level number, and what happened. A screenshot can help, but please remove personal information before sending it. Never send passwords or payment details.

Your progress

Level progress is stored locally on your device. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove that progress. Please contact support before reinstalling to troubleshoot a problem.

Privacy questions

Use the same email address for questions about your information or to make a privacy request. See the privacy policy for details.
